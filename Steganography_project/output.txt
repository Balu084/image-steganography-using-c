If they were meant to be a pair,
If they were always destined for each other,
Then why was I brought so close to her?
Why did you let our paths grow like two trees side by side,
Why did you make us spend time together,
With unspoken words and hidden feelings?
Sharing laughs, sharing gossip…
Why make me addicted to her presence,
If all of it was meant to fade into nothingness?

Now she wears her love for him so effortlessly,
And I stand here, a silent spectator of my own heartbreak.
Why was I chosen to witness this?
